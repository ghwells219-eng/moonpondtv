For grown-ups

The science behind the calm

Moon Pond is made by a movement coach with 15+ years of experience coaching kids in dance and gymnastics. Our wind-down episodes follow the order research points to: let little bodies move first, slow the breath, then settle into the same gentle routine.

Visit Moonlight Reading
1

Move first

In a 2023 preschool trial, rhythmic movement to music helped 3 and 4 year olds build self-regulation skills.

2

Slow the breath

A one-minute breathing video lowered kids' heart rates in a 2021 Stanford study of 342 children.

3

Keep the pace gentle

4-year-olds focused better after a slow-paced show than after a fast, frantic one (Pediatrics, 2011).

4

Same routine, every night

In a 14-country study of 10,000+ families, nightly bedtime routines were linked to longer, easier sleep.
